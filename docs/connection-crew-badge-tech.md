## What it is

Connection Crew's event technology by Covve turns every attendee badge into a way to
connect. One tap connects two people, both receive an intro email, and everyone leaves
with a complete list of who they met. No app, no business cards, no lost follow-ups.

One sentence: Badge technology that makes every introduction at your event stick, paired
with a human team that helps the introductions happen.

One paragraph: Every badge at your event carries a QR code and NFC tap point powered by
Covve. Attendees set up a short profile from their phone in about a minute, right in the
browser, with nothing to download. When two people meet, one of them taps or scans the
badge of the person they're meeting and both are connected on the spot, each receiving an
intro email with the details they chose to share. At the end of the event, every attendee
receives a complete list of their connections so following up is easy. Connection Crew
brings the technology into your event alongside our facilitation team, so the tech
supports real conversations instead of replacing them.

What makes it different from a networking app: it works through the badge people are
already wearing, it takes one tap rather than two, it runs in a browser so adoption is
high, and it ships with a team of trained Connectors on site who help people use it and
help them meet.

## The problem it solves

Most events assume connection will happen because people are in the same room. It
usually doesn't. Attendees stay with people they already know, avoid networking because
it feels transactional, collect business cards they never look at again, and leave with
pages of notes and no new relationships.

For attendees the gap shows up as:

- The awkward start: no easy way to open a conversation with a stranger
- The lost follow-up: a great conversation with no name, no email, no next step
- The LinkedIn scramble: typing names into a search bar in the hallway while the person walks away
- The app problem: networking apps that require a download, a login, and a battery, so most people never open them

For organizers and sponsors the gap shows up as:

- No way to see whether people actually met each other
- Sponsors asking for leads and getting a list of badge scans with no relationship behind them
- Post-event surveys that say "great content, wish I'd met more people"
- Networking breaks that feel like a waiting room

The badge technology closes the follow-up gap. The Connection Crew closes the
awkward-start gap. Together they make the thing people came for, meeting each other,
actually happen.

## How it works

The attendee experience, start to finish:

1. Pick up your badge. It already has a QR code and NFC tap point tied to your registration.
2. Set up your profile. Tap your phone to your own badge or scan the QR code with your phone's camera, then choose what you want to share: name, role, company, LinkedIn, a note about what you're looking for. It takes about a minute, runs in your browser, and there is nothing to download. Two tips we share from stage: don't use private or incognito mode, and scan with your camera rather than a QR reader app.
3. Meet someone. Have the conversation. When you want to stay in touch, tap or scan their badge. One tap connects you both. The person you're meeting doesn't need to do anything.
4. Get the intro. You each receive an intro email with the details the other person chose to share, within moments.
5. Keep going. Edit your profile any time by tapping your own badge again. Look for a Connection Crew teammate in a purple shirt if you get stuck.
6. Leave with your people. At the end of the event you receive a complete list of everyone you connected with, so follow-up takes minutes instead of never happening.

What the Crew adds on the day:

- A one-minute badge activation moment from the main stage, usually with a live demo, so the whole room learns it at once
- Teammates at registration and in the hallways helping people set up profiles and make their first connection
- Connection moments built into the program (an opening activity, table prompts at lunch, a Connection Corner) that give people a reason to tap
- Tech support on site so the organizer's team never has to field a badge question

Optional additions for events that want more: intentional matchmaking that forms groups
or pairs before people arrive, a connection challenge with a leaderboard, and
sponsor-redeemable rewards (connect with ten people, claim a drink at the sponsor's booth).

For organizers:

- Networking becomes a feature you can point to, rather than a break you hope goes well
- Higher adoption than an event app because there is nothing to install; the badge is already on everyone
- Zero lift for your team: Connection Crew handles the stage moment, the hallway support, and the troubleshooting
- Fits any registration flow; badges print with the QR code and tap point included
- A simple measure of relational success: how many connections were made across the event
- Attendees leave with their people, which is what drives "I'm coming back next year"
- Works alongside Connection Crew's facilitated moments (opening activity, Longer Table lunch, Joyful Connection Masterclass, Connection Corner), so the technology and the human experience reinforce each other

For sponsors and exhibitors:

- A sponsorable activation that attendees actually want: "connection powered by [sponsor]"
- Covve Scan for the sponsor's booth team, so every visitor becomes a captured, followed-up lead without a fishbowl of business cards
- Reward mechanics that drive traffic: redeem connections for a coffee, a drink, or a prize at the sponsor's booth
- Visibility in a moment attendees remember, the one where they met someone worth knowing

What an organizer gets when they bring Connection Crew in with the badge technology:

- A connection plan for the event, scoped to the audience, schedule, venue, and budget
- The badge technology configured for the event and live on every badge
- A trained Crew on site, or Connection Champions trained from the organizer's own volunteers for events where the full Crew can't travel
- A debrief after the event with what worked and what to do next time

## Value for attendees

- You never lose a good conversation again. Every person you tap is in your list at the end of the event, with the details they chose to share.
- It takes the pressure off the opening. Tapping a badge is a natural reason to say hello, and a natural way to close a conversation well.
- You decide what you share. Your profile holds only what you add to it, and you can change it any time by tapping your own badge.
- Nothing to download, nothing to remember. Your phone's camera and your badge are the whole system.
- Follow-up happens while the conversation is still warm. The intro email arrives right away, so "let's stay in touch" becomes a real thread rather than a promise.
- There are people to help. Connection Crew teammates are on site to set you up, introduce you to someone, and make sure nobody has to eat lunch alone.

## FAQ for prospective event partners

Do attendees need to download an app? No. Everything runs in the phone's browser.
Attendees tap or scan their badge with their camera and they're in.

Do both people need to tap? No. One tap connects you both. The person being tapped
doesn't need to do anything, and if both people tap anyway nothing breaks; they each get a
second email.

What does an attendee share? Only what they add to their profile: typically name, role,
company, LinkedIn, and a note about what they're looking for. They can edit it any time by
tapping their own badge.

What happens to the data? Each attendee receives their own list of the people they
connected with. The list is for staying in touch with the people they met. Organizers and
sponsors receive what is agreed in the event plan.

How does it work with our registration or badge printing? The QR code and tap point print
on the badge as part of your normal badge process. We'll walk through the specifics with
your registration team early, since this is the step that needs the most lead time.

What size event is this for? It has run at conferences of a few hundred to several hundred
attendees, and it scales up. Larger events and custom features such as a connection
competition or sponsor reward redemption bring in the Covve team to configure them.

What does our team have to do on the day? Very little. Connection Crew runs the one-minute
badge activation from stage, staffs the hallways, and handles tech questions. Your team
points people to us.

Can sponsors be involved? Yes. The badge experience can be sponsor-powered, booth teams
can use Covve Scan for lead capture, and we can add reward mechanics that send attendees
to the sponsor.

Is this only the technology, or do you facilitate too? Both, and we recommend both. The
technology makes connections last; the Crew makes them happen. We can also train your own
volunteers as Connection Champions when the full Crew can't be on site.

What does it cost? It depends on event size, how many Crew teammates are on site, and what
else is in the plan. Reach out and we'll scope it with you.

How far ahead should we talk? Events book three to nine months ahead. The badge printing
step is the long pole, so the earlier the better.
