Why this matters

Resilience is relational.

We're told to be resilient on our own: manage stress, develop grit, bounce back. But much of our resilience doesn't live inside us. It lives between us, in the relationships we've built and the care that moves through them.

Connection creates the pathways. Compassion moves care through them. Repeated action builds shared capacity. That capacity is community resilience, and we build it before we need it.

About

Hansen Hunt

he/him
San Diego, California

I am a community builder, certified facilitator, and connection experience designer on a mission to co-create a deeply connected world by bringing people together.

After years of professional success alongside loneliness and isolation, I made a deliberate decision to change how I was living. Sobriety, endurance running, men's work, and committed support circles transformed my life and shaped the work I do today.

I lead Connection Crew by Covve, a global team of connection professionals working to help one million people feel more connected by 2030. Through Connection Crew, I partner with conferences and event teams to bring intentional connection into the experience rather than leaving it to chance: designing the connection arc, training event teams and volunteers, and facilitating experiences where people feel seen, heard, valued, and understood.

My facilitation practice is grounded in extensive training through YPO Key Associate Forums, years of facilitating men's work with The ManKind Project, and three years leading the Joyful Connection Masterclass. The focus is always the same: building the trust, shared leadership, and mutual support that help groups accomplish difficult things without burning people out.

In San Diego, I co-founded the San Diego Chamber of Connection, co-lead the San Diego Community Builders Network, and co-founded Longer Tables San Diego. Following the Balboa Park Table, Longer Tables San Diego is preparing to bring 3,000 San Diegans together for the Waterfront Park Table in spring 2027. Connection Buddy grows out of that work: a way to help the people who bring people together find the right place, the right person, and the next step.

Closer to home, Sara and I co-created the Mission Beach Neighbor Hang, a monthly neighborhood gathering we have hosted with neighbors since May 2024. What began with coffee and bagels has become a living experiment in how small, repeated moments of connection build trust, compassion, and a community's capacity to care for one another. That experiment is where the idea that resilience is relational came from, and it's the lens for everything above.

I love my fiancée and co-presenter, Sara, my family, men's circles, my teammates, my dog Obi-Wan, tacos, meaningful conversations, and creating spaces where people feel they belong.
